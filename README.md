## Origin

The framework begins from a geometric-spectral approach to the Riemann Hypothesis that starts with the continuous family of polylogarithms

```
f_θ(s) = Li_s(e^{iθ}),   θ ∈ ℝ/2πℤ
```

and the purification identity obtained by coherent summation over roots of unity (excluding the trivial term):

```
η_N(s) = ∑_{k=1}^{N-1} Li_s(e^{2π i k / N}) = (N^{1-s} - 1) ζ(s).
```

Individual members of the family may possess zeros off the critical line; the coherent sum cancels them, so that the non-trivial zeros of η_N coincide with those of ζ. The zeros are embedded geometrically on a cone whose radial coordinate corresponds to Re(s). Finite-N self-adjoint operators H_N whose pure-point spectra track the zeros of η_N are assumed to converge in the strong resolvent sense to a limiting operator lim H. The residual continuous spectrum of lim H (the part orthogonal to the critical zeros of the completed xi-function Ξ) is the central obstruction. An abstract Mourre estimate with the atlas phase generator A is proposed to control or empty that residual continuum. The original reduction states that RH is equivalent to the emptiness (or elementary character) of the residual continuous spectrum together with spectral identification of lim H with Z(Ξ).

## Successive Expansions

The residual continuous spectrum abstraction was applied iteratively to successive domains of mathematical physics. Each time a structure appeared that had no counterpart in the geometric framework, the framework was enlarged until the new structure became native. The resulting object is the following.

### Core geometric object

A dual pair of dynamical, stratified, scale-dependent, observer-parametrized cones (bulk and boundary) linked by a residual-continuum holographic dictionary. Residual continuous spectrum is taken as a primary derived quantum object that carries soft-charge information and anomalous variations subject to descent equations. From this primary residual continuum a dual pair of cones and a pure-point spectrum emerge. The emergent pure-point spectrum is required to coincide with the non-trivial zeros of Ξ. All further structures appear as effective descriptions valid after emergence.

Coordinates on the cones include radial (Re(s)), atlas phase θ, mobility/disorder strength μ, channel label α (asymptotic thresholds), renormalization-group / infrared scale λ, observer/vacuum label ν, and non-perturbative instanton-sector label ι. The residual continuous spectrum is distributed over a landscape or ensemble of possible emergences; residual continuum itself generates a dynamical flow on that landscape and on the measure that selects typical members.

### Conjugate operators and Mourre estimates

A hierarchy of conjugate operators acts on the geometry:

- A – atlas phase generator
- B – radial dilation generator
- C – mobility generator
- D_α – channel-adapted generators
- E – scale / infrared generator

together with the actions induced by changes of observer and by non-perturbative sectors. Mourre estimates with positive constants and compact errors are required for every generator, both fibrewise with respect to the observer label and consistently across the holographic dictionary. The estimates control residual continuum after emergence and remain compatible with quantum fluctuations, anomalies and bootstrap relations.

### Structures carried by residual continuous spectrum

Residual continuous spectrum may:

- produce power-law tails (Price-type decay),
- be partially localized across a mobility edge,
- be stratified by thresholds into asymptotic channels,
- accumulate in the infrared as soft modes requiring coherent dressing,
- carry topological indices that can protect portions of it from removal,
- transform under Bogoliubov maps when the observer or vacuum changes while pure-point data remain invariant,
- store information in the form of soft charges or continuous entanglement data whose conservation is imposed as a unitarity constraint,
- exchange spectral weight with the pure-point spectrum through exponentially small non-perturbative mixing amplitudes,
- support anomalous variations subject to descent equations,
- back-react on the supporting geometry (curving the cones, shifting thresholds and mobility edges, deforming the holographic dictionary),
- participate in a closed bootstrap that mutually determines residual continuum and pure-point spectrum,
- undergo quantum fluctuations together with the geometry itself,
- act as the primary object from which geometry and pure-point spectrum emerge,
- be distributed over a dynamical landscape of emergences and generate flow on that landscape.

Complex scaling of the atlas and radial coordinates is available to reveal resonances. Purification is realized by coherent summation or contour integration over the discrete atlas, extended to averages over disorder, channel projections, soft-mode clouds, observer ensembles, instanton sectors and landscape measures, all required to preserve topological indices, soft charges, information content and anomaly data.

### Spectral identification and the claim

After emergence, quantum fluctuations, bootstrap fixed-point equations, geometric back-reaction, infrared dressing, channel projections, topological constraints, anomaly cancellation, information matching, observer averaging, non-perturbative mixing and landscape dynamics have all been taken into account, the pure-point spectrum is required to coincide with Z(Ξ). Any residual continuous spectrum that survives produces tails, scattering data, entanglement continua or soft-charge contributions that are accounted for by known elementary entire factors of Ξ.

Inside this framework the Riemann Hypothesis is equivalent to the existence of such a primary residual continuous spectrum (distributed over a dynamical landscape of emergences) for which the emergent pure-point spectrum equals Z(Ξ) and all of the listed effective structures hold consistently.

## Status

The framework is a highly elaborated geometric-spectral reduction. Every layer after the original purification identity and the formal Mourre template remains open: the concrete realization of the operators, the verification of the Mourre estimates, the existence of the emergence mechanism, the landscape measure, the quantum consistency conditions, and the final spectral identification have not been established. The construction isolates a precise (and now extremely rich) analytic claim; it does not constitute a proof of the Riemann Hypothesis.

Each time a structural element lacked a counterpart, the geometric framework was enlarged until that element became native.
