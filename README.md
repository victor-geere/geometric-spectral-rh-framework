## 1. Primary object

Let Σ_res denote a **primary residual continuous spectrum**: a derived quantum object (an object of a suitable derived category of sheaves or spectra) that carries the additional structures listed in §3. Σ_res is distributed over a dynamical landscape ℒ of possible emergences, equipped with a measure μ_ℒ and a continuous-time flow generated by Σ_res itself.

Purification is realized by coherent summation / contour integration over the discrete atlas, extended to averages over disorder realizations, channel projections, soft-mode clouds, observer ensembles, instanton sectors and the landscape measure μ_ℒ. ```
∑_{k=1}^{N-1} Li_s(e^{2π i k / N}) = (N^{1-s} - 1) ζ(s).

## 7. Formal claim

**Inside the framework the Riemann Hypothesis is equivalent to the existence of a primary residual continuous spectrum Σ_res (distributed over a dynamical landscape ℒ) that carries all the structures of §3, generates an emergence of dual cones and pure-point spectrum as in §2, satisfies the full hierarchy of Mourre estimates of §4, is compatible with the purification averages of §5, and fulfils the spectral identification of §6.**

## 8. Status

The elementary purification identity is rigorous. Every subsequent layer—the concrete realization of the operators, the verification of the Mourre hierarchy, the existence of the emergence mechanism, the landscape measure and flow, the quantum consistency conditions, and all structures listed in §3 through §6—remains open. The framework isolates a precise analytic claim; it does not constitute a proof of the Riemann Hypothesis.
